No organization is represented as an approved or affiliated partner unless separately confirmed. Do not submit client names, voucher numbers, medical details, or other sensitive information through unverified forms or email.

How referrals will work

  1. Organizations apply and are verified before receiving partner access.
  2. Authorized staff submit referrals using a secure, consent-based workflow.
  3. Suitable available housing is identified using the applicant's housing preferences and applicable program requirements.
  4. Authorized participants receive status updates without unnecessary disclosure of personal information.

A referral is not a guarantee of housing, voucher approval, inspection, or landlord acceptance. Fair housing requirements apply.
