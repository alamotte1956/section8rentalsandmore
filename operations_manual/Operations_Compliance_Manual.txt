1. HOUSING CHOICE VOUCHER (HCV) WORKFLOW:
   - Tenant Match: Ensure voucher bedroom size matches property configuration.
   - HUD-52517 RFTA: Submit completed Request for Tenancy Approval + utility split.
   - Rent Reasonableness: Rent must not exceed unassisted market comparables.
   - NSPIRE Inspection: Property must pass NSPIRE before HAP contract execution.
   - HAP Contract: Executed between Landlord and Housing Authority; monthly direct 
     deposit initiates on the 1st of each month.

2. NSPIRE COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST (TOP FAILURE ITEMS):
   - Smoke Alarms: Working alarm inside every bedroom + within 21 feet outside sleeping areas.
   - Carbon Monoxide: Working CO detector within 10 feet of each sleeping room.
   - Water Heater TPR Valve: Extension pipe within 6 inches of the floor; no threads at outlet.
   - GFCI Outlets: Required within 6 feet of all water sources (sinks, tubs).
   - Egress Windows: Unobstructed opening, functioning locks, clear exit pathways.

3. STATUTORY EVICTION NOTICE TIMELINES:
   - Minnesota: Minn. Stat. § 504B.321 mandates a minimum 14-day written notice with 
     itemized accounting and emergency financial aid disclosures prior to filing eviction.
   - Wisconsin: Wis. Stat. § 704.17 mandates 5-day notice with right to cure (or 14-day 
     notice without right to cure for year leases / repeated violations).
   - CARES Act: 30-day notice required for covered properties participating in federal programs.
   - PHA Notice Copy: Federal regulations (24 C.F.R. § 982.310) require a copy of any 
     eviction notice to be served concurrently on the administering Housing Authority.
